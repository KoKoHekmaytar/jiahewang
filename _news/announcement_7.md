We were invited to give a continuum robotics demonstration at the [**2026 National Summit on Artificial Intelligence**](https://www.naaic.ai/events/national-ai-summit) Student Project Showcase at Chandler-Gilbert Community College.

It was a great afternoon sharing our work with educators and industry professionals, including conversations with Samir Saber and Joe Abeita about the future of continuum robotics and its potential applications. Thank you, Akiko, for the invitation!
