Two research themes, six presentation stops, and a lot of running between rooms. That was my **IROS 2026**.

It was a great experience sharing two recent research directions in data-driven modeling and soft robotics control:

1. **Physics-informed Koopman modeling:** using limited experimental data to learn residual dynamics, correct physics-based model errors, and enable closed-loop control.
2. **Real-time 3D full-body shape control:** controlling the complete shape of a multi-segment soft robot in real time, rather than focusing only on its tip.

Throughout the conference, I had the opportunity to present this work through:

- Main conference oral, robot demonstration, and poster
- Spotlight talk at _When Muscles Think_
- Spotlight presentation at _Sim2Real and Classical Control_
- Oral presentation at _Soft Robotics in Unstructured Environments_
- Lightning talk at _Mechanically Intelligent Soft Robotics_
- Poster at _The SOFT Frontier 4_

I am especially grateful to Dr. Dongting Li, Dr. Dario Sanalitro, Dr. Haitao Qing, and Dr. Wei Wang for the invitations and opportunities to share our work with these communities.

It was great to share our work at IROS alongside my labmates, Rohan Khatavkar and Eric Weissman. We are all members of the [Robotic Actuators and Dynamics Lab](https://sunrobotics.lab.asu.edu/) supervised by [Dr. Jiefeng Sun](https://jiefengsun.github.io/) at Arizona State University.
